Stay safe

Three habits that protect your money.

1

Keep your PIN and OTP to yourself.

Nobody who works at a bank needs them. Never read them out, type them into a link or send them in a message.

2

Turn on 2FA.

Register for two-factor authentication so a stolen password alone cannot open your account.

3

Use the official site.

Type the bank's address yourself, or use the app. Be wary of links in messages, and of illegal forex trading offers.
